README

Design judgement for any AI agent making or reviewing visual work and copy: drawings, illustrations, 3D, screens, flows, infographics, film and writing. v0.11.4 starts from the idea, feeling and tone, makes 4–8 roughs, compares them head-to-head in a fresh context of the same model, and shows the top 2–3 to the owner of the work. Only the winner enters the fix loop. Critiques label rule-backed, intent-backed and exploratory findings, with provenance and confidence. Choose the fix level before changing work; “the original is better” is a valid stop. Energy follows the named tone and genre, not a default ritual. Research and craft keep their evidence grades; taste stays with the owner of the work.

Start here
1. What it is: a rule set and critique loop an agent reads to judge and improve its own visual work.
2. Always load AGENTS.md, core/principles.md and core/critique-loop.md.
3. Then add only the modules the task needs, from core/which-modules.md.

 Style bleed

Each sheet starts with a small thumbnail of the lookbook look doing the influencing, then that look applied to one brief at rising strength. The first four are textless previews: a control render (no look), then 20 and 50%. The last three are finished designs with copy: a base render (full rules, no look), then 20, 50 and 80%. LOOK-1 keeps looks at 20-50% by default; LOOK-2 allows 80% only when the look's structure carries the brief's idea, after a check that its subject hasn't crept in.

Heron Jar: 50% won. 20% only showed the white lines once the prompt dropped the control as a reference, so the oval is a little different from the control. (LB-122 White-line block print; a heron standing in reeds, theme curiosity.)

Beetle Lens: 50% won, and her skin holds at every level. All three versions have one flaw in common: the beetle in the lens is a second beetle, not the one on the leaf. (LB-082 Circle-framed crest; a child with a magnifying glass looking at a beetle, theme play.)
