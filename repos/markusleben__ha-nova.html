README

hanova.app · the 5-minute tour · Try HA NOVA in 1 minute · on a demo house, in Claude or ChatGPT, no install


Talk to your home. Every change checked.


  Connect your AI to Home Assistant. Simple things run right away. Other changes show a preview first. Unlocks, opening a garage door or gate, turning off the alarm, and deletes wait for your OK.
  You OK them on the NOVA page in Home Assistant, or with a tap on your iPhone or Android phone.
  No phone in Claude or ChatGPT? You type a four-digit code (only at Manage everything with codes on). Dashboard and room changes still ask in a window on your computer.



  Works with Claude Code · Claude Desktop · Codex · OpenCode · Google Antigravity · Hermes Agent (preview)
  and in Claude (also on your phone) and ChatGPT on the web, on a computer, with the NOVA Relay App



  📱 Use it from your phone in the Claude app. ChatGPT works on the web, on a computer. No computer has to run HA NOVA.
  See how easy it is


 "When I get home, set my main lights to a warm welcome scene": you say one sentence and get a checked automation, plus ideas you might not have thought of.

 ✦ Three steps to connect

1. Run the installer. Copy one command. Setup opens a page in your browser and finds your Home Assistant and your AI apps.
2. Sign in with Home Assistant. Sign in as the owner: the first account made when Home Assistant was set up. If needed, setup installs and starts the NOVA Relay App for you. Then this computer connects by itself.
3. Choose your safety net, then look around. Unlocks and deletes wait for your OK on your linked phone or the NOVA page; the confirmation window on this computer asks for dashboard and room changes. To add a phone, pick whose phone should confirm on this computer: setup lists the phones Home Assistant tracks for an administrator's person. On a re-run, Use another phone moves it: the old phone OKs the move, then the new phone taps "Allow". If you use Claude Code, its rules are on unless you turn them off: they let Claude Code read HA NOVA's files and make changes you already approved, without asking again. You still confirm every unlock or delete. Then you see your home at a glance: things worth a look (like a low battery or devices without a room) and an automation made for your home, to preview and save. Nothing in your home changes unless you save it.

Signed in, but not as the owner? The page asks the owner to sign in here, or to make a six-digit code for you (details). Over SSH or with --no-browser, setup asks in the terminal instead. No browser here? Then setup shows the NOVA Relay App page (click "Add", then Install and Start; skipped when the App already runs). You pair with a six-digit code: the Home Assistant owner clicks "Connect a device" on the NOVA page in the sidebar, and you type the code. Over SSH, this works well on Linux only. On Windows and macOS, run setup in a terminal on that computer's own screen: over SSH, HA NOVA usually can't save its key.
